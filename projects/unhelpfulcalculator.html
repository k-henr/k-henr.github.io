Unhelpful calculator

This is a pretty normal calculator, with support for variables, functions and a few built-in constants. However, the calculator doesn't like maths and will complain at you if the expression you wrote is too complicated and hard to figure out. You can ask it to try again, and it'll have a slightly higher tolerance for hard maths.
